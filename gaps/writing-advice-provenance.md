**Gap:** `topics/writing.md` was vendored from popular craft sources (Strunk
and White, Orwell, Zinsser, Williams, McPhee, Lamott, King, Le Guin) without
tracing which rules are load-bearing, which are one author's taste, and
which contradict the aims graehl actually holds for authored prose. graehl
has not followed the advice himself and treats all "expert artistic
performance" guidance in this repo as vendored blindly. The audit that
produced `technical-writing` § Priority order found four base bullets
encoding a skim-first reader model and a cadence mannerism (topic-sentence
announcement, signpost sentences, "a short sentence after a long one
lands", inline-only qualification); those are fixed, but the rest of the
file has had no equivalent pass, and the story-craft and drafting bullets
are unexamined.

**Noticed while:** Scoping `writing` as a base layer under
`technical-writing` and reconciling the two.

**Fix sketch:** Reconsider `writing.md` bullet by bullet with three
questions per rule: (1) who says it, and does that source have a record of
communicative success rather than only of giving advice (graehl's stated
bar; Pinker, Gopen and Swan, and Peyton Jones pass, Strunk and White is
contested, Zinsser and Lamott are unknown); (2) what output does a capable
writer produce when following it literally, and is that output something
graehl wants (the failure mode found so far is rules phrased as things to
add); (3) does it conflict with `technical-writing` § Priority order or
with `AGENTS.user.md` § Writing and summary style. Prefer restating a rule
as the invariant it approximates over adding genre overrides. Record
verdicts in `topics/writing.evidence.md` and drop, rewrite, or scope each
rule in place. The Orwell six rules, the paragraph-review procedure, and
the project-records section are the likely survivors; the drafting bullets
(Lamott, King) and "read it aloud" are the least traced. Close when every
bullet has a ledger verdict.
