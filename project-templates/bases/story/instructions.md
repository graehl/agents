Before planning, drafting, or revising a story, screenplay, interactive
fiction, character, or world, read `instructions/story-writing.md` in
addition to the writing guidance; before creating or moving a story's files,
read `instructions/story-project-layout.md` and copy its fill-in sheets from
`instructions/story-starters/`. Treat its structure models and plot catalogs
as a menu for diagnosis, not a form to fill: a short story needs a hero who
wants something, an obstacle, and a turn. A page that is not a story reads
none of this.
