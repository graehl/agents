# Audience

Prefilled storybook default. Change any line; the agent follows this file.

**Who reads this:** kids about the author's age and a little younger, and a
grown-up reading it aloud.
**Age or reading level:** middle grade (about ages 8 to 12) unless the
author says otherwise.
**What they already know:** nothing about this story's world or characters;
everything must be shown as it comes up.
**What they come for:** to find out what happens, and to laugh or shiver
along the way.
**How they read it:** on a phone or tablet in short sits, or read aloud at
bedtime, a chapter at a time.
**What would lose them:** a slow start, long paragraphs, words they do not
know with no help from the sentence, a chapter that ends without a reason
to turn the page.

## Consequences

- Sentence and paragraph length: short sentences, paragraphs of one to four
  sentences, plenty of dialogue.
- Vocabulary help: a new word is explained by the sentence around it, never
  by a footnote.
- Content bar: all ages; scary is fine, cruelty shown for spectacle is not.
- Illustration role: pictures decorate and mark chapters; the words carry
  the story.
- Chapters: short, each ending on a reason to keep reading.
