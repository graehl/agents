
## This is a storybook

The story comes first. Tell your agent who it is about and what they want,
and it will help you write a short version all the way to the end, then make
it longer from the part you like best. It will not bother you about how the
page looks or about pictures until you say the story is done.

Three small files at the top of the project are already filled in with good
defaults: `audience.md` (who this is for), `prose-style.md` (how the writing
should sound) and `illustration-style.md` (what kind of pictures). Change
any line and the agent follows. The story itself lives in `story/`.

When the story is done, the agent will put it on the page and ask about the
look, the pictures, and whether you want a printed or read-aloud version.
Ask for a **remix** (Codex: `$remix`) to get the same story for a younger
reader, in first person, or with different pictures, kept beside the
original. Ask for a **riff** (Codex: `$riff`) any time you want four
different versions of something to choose from.
