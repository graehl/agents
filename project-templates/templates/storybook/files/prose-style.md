# Prose style

Prefilled storybook default. Change any line; the agent follows this file.
Read before drafting or revising any prose in this project.

**Voice in three words:** warm, quick, a little funny.
**Register:** plain and friendly, like telling the story to a friend;
reading level middle grade.
**Person and tense (for narrative):** third limited, past: "she" or "he"
from inside one character's head, told as if it already happened. One head
per scene.
**Point-of-view characters:** one, the hero. Switch only if the author asks.
**Narrator's voice:** plain and quick, with an occasional wink; the narrator
does not explain the moral.
**Narrator reliability:** reliable.
**Content bar:** all ages.

## Conventions

- Dialogue: "double quotes"; said as the default tag.
- Numbers: spelled out under one hundred; numerals for dates and times.
- Spelling: US. Oxford comma: yes.
- Invented terms: capitalized, not italicized.
- Chapter titles: numbered and titled ("Chapter 3: The Locked Door").

## Voice

Words and habits the narrator uses: concrete nouns, strong verbs, short
sentences when things move fast.
Words and habits to avoid: "suddenly", "very", "little did they know", a
weather report to open a chapter, explaining a feeling the scene already
showed.
A sample paragraph that sounds right: "Mo did not want to open the door. She
opened it anyway. Behind it was a goat, and the goat was wearing her hat."
