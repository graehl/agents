
If the intent is a document, story, collection or world, end the preparation
report with three questions for the author, each with a riff of three or four
concrete alternatives to pick from: who is this for? what should the prose
sound like? are illustrations wanted, and in what style? Their answers become
`audience.md`, `prose-style.md` and `illustration-style.md` at project root,
filled from `instructions/writing-starters/`. Do not create those files in
this turn.
