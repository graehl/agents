
If the intent is a document, story, collection or world, end the preparation
report with two questions for the author, each with a riff of three or four
concrete alternatives to pick from: what should the prose sound like, and are
illustrations wanted, and in what style? Their answers become `prose-style.md`
and `illustration-style.md` at project root, filled from
`instructions/writing-starters/`. Do not create either file in this turn.
