# Provisional questions for creative steering and transfer

**Grounded initial overlay, 2026-09-27.** This records what the
[survey](survey.md) leaves unresolved. No novelty ranking or run is authorized.

## Can source effects survive equal opportunities to use randomness?

SSoT's Appendix D.6 already supplies an internal/external comparison, so that
broad contrast is not a void. Its tool reliability and single-external-string
conditions leave room for a matched workflow comparison. The cheapest
discriminating follow-up would fix length/alphabet, repeated access, recipient,
and generation budget. Then test panel utility on new briefs. This is a
proposal, not evidence that matching these factors removes the reported gain.

## Can a creative tendency pass through strings without training?

The incumbent evidence is mixed in intervention type: fine-tuning transfers
traits in Cloud et al.; their ICL arm fails; selected numeric tokens steer
preferences in Zur et al. None establishes the proposed cross-recipient
creative-style channel. A frozen output readout with generator/recipient
crossings, context-only controls, and held-out strings/briefs is a discriminating
test. A positive attribution result still needs a separate creative-value test.

## Does a predictive internal trace improve distillation?

Attention sharing can alter image style, and output-distribution distillation
can internalize prompted behavior. Neither establishes that the most predictive
style trace is the best transfer target. Compare output-only distillation with
added selected and unrelated trace targets, evaluating styles independently of
the selection probe. No source in this initial corpus closes that question;
the internal-representation-distillation neighborhood remains undercovered.

The cited sources control their own experiments; no local reproduction or
independent confirmation was established in this pass. Revisit these questions
when choosing an experiment or when cross-lineage/trace-transfer evidence
arrives. An unscheduled coverage reminder is 2026-12-01; no automatic job exists.
