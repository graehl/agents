# Distillation survey vocabulary

| term | definition | topic / refs |
|---|---|---|
| annotation-only teacher | The access regime used in this map: emitted labels are available, while teacher hidden states and task-label distributions are not. This does not exclude student-side probability diagnostics. | [teacher access](survey.md#teacher-access-determines-the-available-methods) |
| recoverability prediction | A prediction, frozen before an intervention, of which student errors will improve under that intervention. Retrospective correlation with current errors alone does not qualify. | [frontier](frontier.md) |
| shared-background reading | A concept and its committed source extract reused from another survey, without duplicating extraction or claiming an independent replication. | [structure and representation](survey.md#structure-and-representation-supervision) |
