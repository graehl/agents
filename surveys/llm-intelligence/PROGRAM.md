# Program

Map the mechanisms and evidence bearing on what language models represent,
compute, use, and can report about their internal state.

The program emphasizes causal and discriminating comparisons among internal
representation, lenses and probes, steering, circuit tracing, latent versus
verbalized reasoning, and self-access. It uses J-space as an anchor without
letting one paper define the field, distinguishes functional access and
reportability from phenomenal-consciousness claims, and treats interpretability
methods as bounded instruments whose causal force, model regime, and plausible
falsifiers must remain explicit.
