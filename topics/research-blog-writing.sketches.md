# Research-blog-writing sketches

> Extreme-draft, observed-but-untested form preferences and narrative cadence
> for a selective research showcase.

Topic: `research-blog-writing`

Status: **EXTREME DRAFT / UNTESTED** — inferred from research blogs the user
has seen; this project has not authored one.

## Paper forms that may travel well

Research blogs seem especially compatible with forms whose reader gift is
immediate and demonstrable:

- headline result or simple-baseline reversal;
- intervention ladder or recipe synthesis;
- diagnostic instrument or synthetic microscope;
- research engine with checkable discoveries; and
- resource release with one accessible worked example.

This is a tendency, not an exclusion rule. A theorem, audit, failure atlas, or
other form can anchor a compelling post when its payoff can be shown clearly.

## Candidate cadence

1. Show the coolest representative output, demo, or result first.
2. State what changed and the narrow regime in which it worked.
3. Give the shortest mechanism or recipe that lets a reader understand or try
   it.
4. Contrast the prior or baseline with the same visual grammar.
5. Bound the claim and link the rigorous paper, handout, code, data, or logs.

Consult [`paper-attractiveness`](paper-attractiveness.md) deliberately: vivid
examples, stakes, released assets, and repeated visual grammar are central to
the genre. They still communicate only what the evidence supports.
