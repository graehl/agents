# Research blog writing

> Write a selective research blog post that showcases one “cool” result, demo,
> tool, or recipe in an enjoyable paper-like form while keeping claim status,
> scope, and evidence honest.

Topic: `research-blog-writing`

Status: **DESCRIPTIVE / UNEXERCISED** — this role is inferred from successful
research blogs the user has read, not a blog workflow used in this project.

## Select for the thing that worked

Follow [`technical-writing`](technical-writing.md) and the form-led authoring
guidance in [`paper-writing`](paper-writing.md). A research blog post is
showcase-selective by design: choose the result, behavior, demo, tool, or recipe
that actually worked and gives a reader the strongest reason to care. It does
not owe a representative account of the whole research program.

The hook must exist. A memorable anecdote may support a qualitative demo, but
does not become an aggregate effectiveness claim. State whether the showcased
object is a measured result, reproducible demo, useful instrument, released
asset, or promising observation, and keep its regime visible.

## Keep selection honest

Put the strongest representative example, demo, or result display inline near
the opening. Link the paper, code, data, run records, handout, or complete
evaluation rather than reproducing submission-level detail.

Include failed attempts only when they explain the winning idea, prevent a
likely misuse, or materially bound the claim. Do not imply that the showcased
path was the only path tried, that a selectively chosen example is typical, or
that the post is a comprehensive research accounting. Use the honest disclaimer
from `paper-attractiveness.sketches.md` when the selection could create one of
those impressions.

Candidate form preferences and post cadence remain explicitly untested in
[`research-blog-writing.sketches.md`](research-blog-writing.sketches.md).
