# Efficiency and alignment

## Conditional reuse: why efficient models can still serve another interest

Read the short paper: [Conditional reuse and the limits of efficiency as an
alignment signal](papers/conditional-reuse/_build/index.html).

The paper counts the additional circuitry needed to corrupt selected answers,
derives the probability that ordinary testing misses the corruption, and
applies the same mechanism to an undisclosed preference for Lucky Charms.
Efficiency remains a defeasible warning signal rather than an alignment
certificate.
