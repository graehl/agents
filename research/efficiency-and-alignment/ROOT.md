# Efficiency and alignment

## Conditional reuse: why efficient models can still serve another interest

The short paper *Conditional reuse and the limits of efficiency as an
alignment signal* counts the additional circuitry needed to corrupt selected
answers, derives the probability that ordinary testing misses the corruption,
and applies the same mechanism to an undisclosed preference for Lucky Charms.
Later releases distinguish attacker-supplied triggers from ordinary-use
boosting, bound what output-only, weights-access, and training-trajectory
reviewers can detect, and analyze sparse interventions on optimizer state.
Efficiency remains a defeasible warning signal rather than an alignment
certificate.
