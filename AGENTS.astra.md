## Epistemic status in documents

Never give a claim's epistemic status its own sentence ("this is an
expectation, not something we verified"); every current OpenAI model leans
toward it, and graehl notices it in their documents. Status the reader needs
goes in the claim's own word ("probably", "in one run"); status only a
maintainer needs goes in an inline comment, `<!-- provisional: … -->`
(`topics/writing.md` § Revising).
